Standard volumes (m³) 家具の標準容積

Approximate truck load volume per item for customer furniture lists. It never sets a price or job time.

Researched values come from Australian removalists' published volume tables (checked {{ checked_on }}). Matching values → median; one value → that value; none → provisional. Product sizes (outer volume) are shown separately and are not used.

Changing a value applies to items added from now on. Items already in a customer's list keep the m³ they were added with. 変更は今後追加される荷物にのみ適用され、既存リストの数値は変わりません。
