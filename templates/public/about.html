JR Booking System

JR Booking System is the private, internal booking system of {{ company.company_name }} ({{ company.company_legal_name }}), a removals company in Perth, Western Australia. Only {{ company.company_name }}'s owner and staff use it, to manage removal jobs: bookings, schedules, crew and trucks, invoices and payments.

What it does with Google

  • Gmail (read-only) — the company account {{ gmail_address }} is connected with read-only access. Every 5 minutes the system looks at emails the company has sent that say “Confirmed”, reads that email conversation and creates a draft booking with the status Check to Confirm. The owner checks and edits it, then confirms it. The system never sends, changes, moves or deletes email.
  • Google Calendar — when a booking is confirmed, the system can add or update an event in the company's own calendar so the team can see the job.

Customers do not sign in to this system, and it is not offered to other businesses.

Privacy: read the JR Booking System privacy policy — what Google data is read, why, how it is stored, who it is shared with and how to have it deleted.
