Privacy policy — JR Booking System

Effective {{ effective_date }} · {{ company.company_legal_name }} trading as {{ company.company_name }}, Perth, Western Australia

JR Booking System (“the system”) is the internal booking system of {{ company.company_name }}. Only the company's owner and staff use it. This policy explains which Google user data the system accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how it can be deleted. It also covers the customer details the system keeps for removal jobs.

Limited Use. JR Booking System's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Google accounts that are connected

Only the company's own Google accounts are connected, by the owner:

  • Company Gmail — {{ gmail_address }}, permission gmail.readonly (read only).
  • Google Calendar — the company calendar, permission calendar.events.

Customers and other people never sign in with Google to this system.

2. What Gmail data is read

The system reads email only from the connected company account, and only:

  • emails the company has sent that contain the word “Confirmed”, sent after the owner turned the check on (older email is never imported);
  • for each such email, the other messages in the same conversation (thread), and up to three older conversations from the last 180 days that mention the same customer's email address (for example the original quote request).

From these messages it reads the sender, recipients, subject, date, the text written in each message and the names of attachments. Attachments themselves are not opened or downloaded. Other email in the mailbox is not read. The system never sends, changes, labels, marks as read, moves or deletes any email.

3. Why it is used

Gmail data is used only to prepare booking records for {{ company.company_name }}'s own removal jobs: to tell whether a sent email confirms a booking, a change, a cancellation or only a payment, and to fill in the booking details (customer name, email, phone, moving date and start time, pickup and delivery address, number of movers, hourly rate, call-out, additional charges, furniture and notes). New bookings are created as Check to Confirm; the owner reviews and edits every one before confirming it. Details the email does not state are left empty, not guessed.

Google Calendar is used only to create, update or remove calendar events for confirmed bookings (customer name, job time, addresses, crew and notes) so the team can see the schedule.

Google data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles. It is not used to train artificial-intelligence models. People only read it when needed for the booking (the company owner and staff), for security, or where the law requires.

4. How it is stored and protected

What is storedThe booking fields listed above (as an ordinary booking); for each processed email: the Gmail message and conversation IDs, sent time, subject, the customer's email address, a one-line summary and the extracted details; the Google sign-in token. The full text of emails is not stored.
WhereA PostgreSQL database hosted by Render (Oregon, United States). The Google sign-in token is kept in that database, never in source code or files.
ProtectionEncrypted connections (HTTPS) only; the system is behind a staff login; secrets are kept in the hosting provider's protected settings.

5. Sharing with third parties

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — to extract the booking details, the text of the email conversation is sent to Anthropic's API and the answer is returned to the system. Anthropic processes it as a service provider for this purpose only and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models.
  • Render — hosts the system and its database.
  • Google — provides Gmail and Google Calendar.

Email content is not shared with anyone else. After the owner confirms a booking it is handled like any other booking (for example an invoice in Xero, a card payment with Stripe, or a text message to the customer through Twilio when the owner uses those features); email content itself is never sent to those services. Google user data is not sold or transferred for any other purpose.

6. How long it is kept and how to delete it

  • Stop access — the owner can disconnect the company Gmail in the system (Settings → Company Gmail → Disconnect), which deletes the stored token, and can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Email details — “Clear stored email details” (Settings → Company Gmail) removes the subject, customer email address, summary and extracted details of every processed email. Deleting a booking also removes those details for the emails linked to it. Only the Gmail message ID is kept so that the same email is never imported twice.
  • Bookings — kept as business records for as long as Australian law requires (generally five years for tax records), then deleted on request.
  • Requests — customers and anyone else can ask to see, correct or delete their information by contacting us (below). We reply within 30 days.

7. Customer information

For removal jobs the system keeps customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, job details, invoices and payment status. It is used only to provide the removal service and is handled under the Australian Privacy Principles.
