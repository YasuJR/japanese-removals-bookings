Bank transfers

Import a Westpac (or other bank) CSV. Invoice numbers are read from Description / Narrative and Reference. Formats such as INV25, Inv25, inv25, INV 25, and Inv 25 all match. When the credit amount equals the invoice total, the invoice is marked Paid and the booking becomes Completed. Amount mismatches stay unpaid and show as Payment mismatch on the Dashboard.

Westpac columns: Date, Narrative, Debit Amount, Credit Amount. Generic CSVs may use Transaction date, Description, Reference, Amount.
